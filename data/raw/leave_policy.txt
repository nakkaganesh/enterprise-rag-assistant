EMPLOYEE LEAVE AND BENEFITS POLICY

Policy HR-2026-201 defines employee leave entitlements and procedures.

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Employees must submit planned leave requests through the company HR system at least five working days in advance whenever possible.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of five days into the following calendar year.

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

Sick leave lasting more than three consecutive working days requires appropriate medical documentation.

Parental leave is provided according to applicable employment laws and company benefits guidelines.

Employees should contact the Human Resources department for questions regarding leave eligibility or exceptional circumstances.
