
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

The Employee Handbook provides general workplace guidelines for company employees.

Standard working hours are determined by an employee's role, team, and employment agreement.

Employees are expected to maintain accurate attendance and working-time records where required.

Employees should communicate workplace concerns to their manager or Human Resources.

Company policies covering remote work, leave, information security, travel, expenses, technology use, and professional conduct apply to all eligible employees.

Employees are responsible for reviewing applicable company policies and complying with current requirements.

Questions regarding employment policies should be directed to Human Resources.
