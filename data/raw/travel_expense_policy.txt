
TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

Policy FIN-2026-301 defines company travel and expense reimbursement requirements.

Employees must obtain manager approval before booking business travel.

Air travel should normally be booked in economy class.

Hotel expenses are reimbursable up to the limits established for the destination.

Meals incurred during approved business travel may be reimbursed when supported by valid receipts.

Individual expenses above ₹5,000 require manager approval.

Employees must submit expense claims within 15 days after completing business travel.

Personal expenses are not reimbursable by the company.
