
EMPLOYEE CODE OF CONDUCT

Policy ETH-2026-401 defines expected professional behaviour for all employees.

Employees must act honestly, respectfully, and professionally in the workplace.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and retaliation are prohibited.

Employees must avoid conflicts between personal interests and company responsibilities.

Confidential company or customer information must not be disclosed without authorization.

Suspected misconduct should be reported to a manager, Human Resources, or the appropriate compliance channel.

Retaliation against an employee who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.
