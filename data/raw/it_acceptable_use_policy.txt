
IT ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

Policy IT-2026-501 governs acceptable use of company technology resources.

Company computers, networks, email accounts, and software are primarily provided for authorized business activities.

Limited personal use of company devices is permitted when it does not interfere with work, violate company policy, or create security risks.

Employees must not install unauthorized software on company devices.

Employees must protect company devices from unauthorized access.

Company technology must not be used for illegal, abusive, discriminatory, or malicious activities.

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported immediately to the IT department.
